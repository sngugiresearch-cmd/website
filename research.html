Research

I study the internal mechanisms of language models — how facts are encoded and edited, how features entangle, and how models represent uncertainty about themselves.

Current direction

Investigating metacognition circuits in LLMs — whether models encode internal representations of their own knowledge boundaries. Broader interests: polysemanticity, circuit analysis, and safe knowledge editing.

arXiv preprint Aug 2025 knowledge-editing PEFT

Surgical Knowledge Rewrite in Compact LLMs: An 'Unlearn-then-Learn' Strategy with ((IA)³) for Localized Factual Modulation and Catastrophic Forgetting Mitigation

A two-stage, circuit-localized knowledge editing strategy using (IA)³ on Phi-3-mini. Achieves 98.50% accuracy on the modulated fact while suppressing the original conflicting fact (96%), with unprecedented localization (72.00% F_control accuracy) versus ~20% for direct fine-tuning. Qualitative analysis reveals a "soft forgetting" mechanism where suppressed knowledge remains latent but conditionally accessible.

arXiv preprint Jun 2025 cross-lingual-alignment LoRA

Targeted Lexical Injection: Unlocking Latent Cross-Lingual Alignment in Lugha-Llama via Early-Layer LoRA Fine-Tuning

Demonstrates that Lugha-Llama-8B-wura already encodes near-perfect Swahili–English lexical alignment at Layer 2 (cosine similarity 0.99998) that is lost by the output layer (baseline 0.32). TLI fine-tunes toward this early-layer signal with a contrastive LoRA objective, improving output-level alignment +28% on trained pairs and generalizing +28% to unseen controls (p < 7.17 × 10⁻²⁷).

Oct 2025 polysemanticity SAEs

Taming Incidental Polysemanticity in Toy Models: How Network Training Choices Affect Feature Entanglement

Exploratory toy-model study measuring feature entanglement with sparse autoencoders across training configurations (L1/L2 regularization, orthogonal init, activation functions, noise). L2-trained networks produced 17.9% less polysemantic representations than L1 (t=2.8, p<0.01), suggesting training dynamics — not just SAE architecture — shape interpretability outcomes.
